Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

arXiv.gg helps you discover research papers. You can search and browse public papers without signing in.

Accounts and research activity

If you sign in with Google, we use account information, including your email address and profile information, to create and operate your account. Session cookies keep you signed in. Signed-in paper views can be associated with your account to support your research trail. Feedback you choose to submit is stored so that we can review and respond to it.

Analytics and service operation

We use Google Analytics on public discovery pages to understand visits and interactions, including searches and paper links. Analytics can use cookies and process device, browser, and usage information. Service providers that host and protect this site may process network information such as IP addresses and request logs.

For Google's data practices, see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

We are preparing this site for Google AdSense review. Ownership verification alone does not display ads or load advertising scripts. Before enabling advertising, we will configure the applicable consent choices and update this notice to describe the advertising in use.

Your choices and contact

You can browse without an account, sign out, and manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect sign-in. For privacy questions or a request concerning your account information, email lyndon@lambda.run.
