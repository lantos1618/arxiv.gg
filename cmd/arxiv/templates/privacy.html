Privacy

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

arXiv.gg helps you discover research papers. You can search and browse public papers without signing in.

Accounts and research activity

If you sign in with Google, we use account information, including your email address and profile information, to create and operate your account. Session cookies keep you signed in. Signed-in paper views can be associated with your account to support your research trail. Feedback you choose to submit is stored so that we can review and respond to it.

Analytics and service operation

We use Google Analytics on public discovery pages to understand visits and interactions, including searches and paper links. Analytics cookies are off until you choose “Allow analytics cookies” in our prompt. You can change your choice using “Analytics cookies” in the footer. We store that preference in your browser so it applies across public pages. Choosing “Keep cookies off” also removes this site's Google Analytics cookies.

With analytics cookies off, Google receives limited, cookieless measurement pings, which can include the page URL, referrer, browser information, timestamp, and consent state. Advertising storage remains denied in our Analytics configuration, and we disable Google signals and advertising personalization in Analytics. Account, sign-in, admin, and API pages do not load Analytics. Service providers that host and protect this site may process network information such as IP addresses and request logs.

For Google's data practices, see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
